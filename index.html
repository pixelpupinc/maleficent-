Intention

Malevolence is the alarm. Maleficence is the instrument.

The aim is not cruelty. The aim is refusal: refusal to let rot call itself peace, refusal to let confusion pass as mystery, refusal to let access continue after the terms have been violated.

In this frame, maleficence is disciplined severity. It does not hunt for a body. It cuts a pattern, closes a door, names a distortion, and returns power to the living center.

No person is the target.

The dead pattern is the target.

Reasoning

Why this is necessary

A living system has to reject what poisons it. If love is never allowed to say no, love becomes a resource other people mine until nothing is left.

Anger marks the breach. Frustration marks the obstruction. Justice restores proportion. The violet reaction is the moment those forces stop looping inside the body and become visible structure.

Justice

Chop up the dead stuff

The blade is symbolic. It is the decision to cut stale agreements, rotten access, false peace, inherited guilt, and any story that asks the living self to keep feeding what has already died.

1 Notice the rot

Name what drains, delays, or distorts.

2 Cut cleanly

Remove access, obligation, and false consent.

3 Compost the lesson

Keep the wisdom, not the wound as a home.

4 Grow forward

Let the living thing take back space.
